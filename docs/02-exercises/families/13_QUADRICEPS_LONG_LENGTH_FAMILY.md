# Quadriceps Long-Length Family

## Status

**Validated family draft — includes one historical EVO position plus modern extensions.**

## Family role

This family separates long-length quadriceps loading from the broader squat family. It is especially useful for targeting knee-extension demand without requiring the same whole-body balance and hip contribution as a squat or lunge.

## Primary versions

### A. Standing Straight-Leg Raise Iso
- **Type:** Targeted / integrated hybrid
- **Provenance:** Schroeder / EVO core-associated
- **Use:** Historical quadriceps-focused position

### B. Supported Sissy-Squat Iso
- **Type:** Integrated
- **Provenance:** Schroeder-derived / later associated use
- **Use:** Advanced long-length quadriceps loading

### C. Strap Knee-Extension Iso
- **Type:** Targeted
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Precise knee-extension force at a selected joint angle

## Standing Straight-Leg Raise Iso

### Historical anchor

Surviving Schroeder-associated material describes the standing straight-leg raise as a quadriceps-focused Iso-Extreme and emphasizes a roughly 90-degree relationship between the raised thigh and torso.

### Setup

- Stand tall with support available for balance.
- Lift one leg in front while keeping the knee extended or nearly extended.
- Bring the thigh toward roughly perpendicular to the torso only if that range can be maintained without lumbar rounding or hip pinching.
- Keep the stance leg stable and pelvis level.
- Avoid leaning backward to manufacture apparent hip-flexion range.

### Active intent

Actively maintain knee extension and continue trying to raise the leg farther while the trunk remains still. The hold should feel muscular through the quadriceps/hip-flexor complex rather than like passive joint compression.

### Learn Mode

1. Establish single-leg standing balance with hand support.
2. Lock in a tall trunk and level pelvis.
3. Raise the working leg gradually.
4. Maintain knee extension.
5. Find the highest clean position without leaning back.
6. Create low-level upward-pull intent through the thigh.
7. Add strong quadriceps tension to keep the knee straight.
8. Hold 8–15 seconds cleanly.
9. Lower under control.

### Workout-mode cues

**Stand tall • knee long • pull the thigh higher without leaning**

## Supported Sissy-Squat Iso

### Setup

- Use stable hand support.
- Rise onto the forefeet as needed and allow the knees to travel forward while the body remains in a controlled line.
- Descend only to a range that creates strong quadriceps loading without knee pain.
- Keep hips from folding backward into a normal squat pattern.

### Active intent

Maintain the long quadriceps position while actively trying to extend the knees without visibly rising.

### Learn Mode

1. Learn supported heel rise and knee travel.
2. Keep torso and thighs organized as one controlled line.
3. Descend gradually.
4. Find a muscular, pain-free knee angle.
5. Build knee-extension force.
6. Hold 6–10 seconds cleanly.
7. Use support to exit smoothly.

### Workout-mode cues

**Stay long • knees forward • extend without rising**

---

## Strap Knee-Extension Iso

Secure the strap so knee extension is resisted at a chosen angle. This allows precise targeted quadriceps work and easy manipulation of joint angle and intent.

### Workout-mode cues

**Thigh fixed • knee angle fixed • extend hard into the strap**

## Rationale

The straight-leg raise has historical Schroeder support. The sissy-squat and strap versions expand the family while remaining clearly labeled according to provenance.
