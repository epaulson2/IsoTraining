# Shoulder Rotation / Stabilization Family

## Status

**Validated family draft — evidence-based extension.**

## Family role

This family adds targeted rotator-cuff and shoulder-position control that is not explicitly covered by the classic EVO core sequence. It is designed to complement, not replace, the integrated demands of push-ups, dips, rows and hangs.

## Primary versions

### A. Strap External-Rotation Iso
- **Type:** Targeted
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** External-rotator strength and joint-position control

### B. Strap Internal-Rotation Iso
- **Type:** Targeted
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Internal-rotator strength and balanced shoulder loading

### C. Overhead Shoulder-Stability Iso
- **Type:** Integrated / targeted hybrid
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Active overhead stabilization with reduced dependence on hanging strength

## Strap External-Rotation Iso

### Setup

- Secure the strap at an angle that resists external rotation.
- Begin with the elbow near the side of the torso and flexed around 90 degrees.
- Place a small towel between elbow and ribs if that helps maintain position.
- Keep wrist neutral and shoulder blade organized without exaggerated retraction.

### Active intent

Attempt to rotate the forearm outward against the immovable strap while keeping the elbow and trunk still.

### Learn Mode

1. Set strap height and length.
2. Position elbow comfortably near the torso.
3. Establish neutral wrist and forearm.
4. Organize ribs and scapula.
5. Build low-level external-rotation force.
6. Increase force without letting the elbow drift.
7. Hold 6–10 seconds cleanly.
8. Reduce force gradually before exiting.

### Workout-mode cues

**Elbow fixed • wrist neutral • rotate outward into the strap**

---

## Strap Internal-Rotation Iso

Use the same geometry but reverse the force direction, attempting to rotate the forearm inward against the strap.

### Workout-mode cues

**Elbow fixed • ribs quiet • rotate inward into the strap**

---

## Overhead Shoulder-Stability Iso

### Setup

- Use a strap, wall or support to hold the arm in a comfortable overhead angle.
- Keep ribs stacked over pelvis.
- Avoid forcing end-range shoulder flexion.
- Maintain active scapular upward-rotation/support rather than passive hanging.

### Active intent

Create multi-directional shoulder stability while preserving the overhead angle. The goal is controlled co-contraction, not maximal movement into end range.

### Learn Mode

1. Find comfortable overhead range.
2. Stack ribs and pelvis.
3. Establish active shoulder support.
4. Add gentle external-rotation / upward-rotation intent.
5. Increase tension without shrugging or arching.
6. Hold 8–15 seconds cleanly.

### Common compensations

- elbow drifting;
- wrist substitution;
- trunk rotation;
- shrugging aggressively;
- rib flare / lumbar extension;
- forcing painful end range.

## Rationale

Shoulder rotation is included for completeness and joint-control balance. This family is an extension based on established isometric principles rather than a Schroeder-original category.
