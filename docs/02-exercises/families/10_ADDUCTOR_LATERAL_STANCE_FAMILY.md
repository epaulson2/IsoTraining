# Adductor / Lateral-Stance Family

## Status

**Validated family draft — evidence-based extension.**

## Family role

This family adds frontal-plane loading and long-length adductor work that is largely absent from the classic EVO sequence.

## Primary versions

### A. Supported Lateral-Lunge / Cossack Iso
- **Type:** Integrated
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Adductor length plus whole-body frontal-plane stabilization

### B. Copenhagen-Style Adductor Iso
- **Type:** Targeted / integrated hybrid
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Higher-intent adductor loading with trunk stabilization

### C. Strap Adduction Iso
- **Type:** Targeted
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Precise adductor force at a selected hip angle

## Supported Lateral-Lunge / Cossack Iso

### Setup

- Take a wide stance with toes generally forward or slightly turned out as hip anatomy permits.
- Shift toward one leg while the opposite leg remains more extended.
- Keep the working foot fully supported.
- Allow the pelvis to travel back and down rather than collapsing sideways into the knee.
- Use a rail, rack or doorframe during learning so balance does not determine depth.

### Active intent

At the chosen depth, actively try to pull the body deeper toward the working side while maintaining foot pressure and pelvis control. The lengthened adductor of the straighter leg should remain active rather than becoming a passive stretch.

### Learn Mode

1. Establish a comfortable wide stance.
2. Learn the lateral weight shift with hand support.
3. Keep the working knee tracking with the foot.
4. Find a tolerable adductor-length position on the straighter leg.
5. Maintain active pressure through both feet.
6. Add low-level pulling intent toward the working side.
7. Increase adductor and hip tension without collapsing.
8. Hold 8–15 seconds cleanly.
9. Return to center under control.

### Common compensations

- working knee collapsing inward;
- foot rolling to an edge;
- pelvis twisting open;
- rounding aggressively to chase depth;
- passive groin stretching without muscular tension.

### Workout-mode cues

**Wide base • knee tracks foot • actively pull deeper without collapsing**

## Copenhagen-Style Adductor Iso

Use a bench/support to load the upper leg in adduction while the trunk remains braced. Begin with shorter lever positions and progress gradually; this is not required for beginners.

### Workout-mode cues

**Stack the trunk • drive support leg down • keep pelvis level**

---

## Strap Adduction Iso

Anchor or route the non-elastic strap so the working leg attempts to pull inward against an immovable endpoint. Select a hip-abducted position that creates useful adductor length without groin pinching.

### Workout-mode cues

**Pelvis square • leg angle fixed • pull inward into the strap**

## Rationale

The frontal plane deserves a dedicated family because whole-body sport and daily movement are not exclusively sagittal. These exercises are transparent extensions, not Schroeder-original positions.
