# Elbow Flexion / Biceps Family

## Status

**Validated family draft. Historical preacher-curl position retained; strap variants are extensions.**

## Family role

This family covers elbow-flexor isometrics with both the classic preacher-style position and adjustable strap loading.

## Primary versions

### A. Preacher Curl Extreme Iso
- **Type:** Targeted / supported
- **Provenance:** Schroeder / EVO core
- **Use:** Classic elbow-flexor position

### B. Strap Curl Iso
- **Type:** Targeted
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Adjustable elbow angle and resistance intent

### C. Long-Length Strap Curl
- **Type:** Targeted
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Emphasize elbow flexors at a more extended elbow angle

## Preacher Curl Extreme Iso

### Setup

- Support the upper arm on a preacher-style surface or equivalent stable pad.
- Choose an elbow angle that creates meaningful biceps loading without locking into painful end-range extension.
- Keep the shoulder from rolling forward excessively.
- Keep wrist neutral or only slightly extended.

### Active intent

Attempt to curl harder while the forearm remains essentially stationary. The support should stabilize the upper arm so the effort stays primarily at the elbow rather than becoming a shoulder movement.

### Learn Mode

1. Set upper-arm contact on the pad.
2. Find a pain-free elbow angle.
3. Align wrist and forearm.
4. Keep shoulder quiet and chest relaxed.
5. Build low-level curl force.
6. Increase intent without lifting the upper arm.
7. Hold 6–10 seconds cleanly.
8. Release force gradually before leaving the position.

### Workout-mode cues

**Upper arm fixed • wrist quiet • curl hard without moving**

## Strap Curl Iso

### Setup

- Stand on or anchor a non-elastic strap securely.
- Adjust length to set the desired elbow angle.
- Keep shoulders down and upper arms close to the chosen position.
- Avoid leaning backward to create apparent force.

### Active intent

Curl the handles upward against the immovable strap.

### Learn Mode

1. Set anchor and strap length.
2. Choose elbow angle.
3. Align wrist and forearm.
4. Stabilize shoulder and trunk.
5. Build curl force gradually.
6. Hold 6–10 seconds without changing posture.

### Common compensations

- shoulder flexion replacing elbow flexion;
- leaning backward;
- wrist flexion;
- elbow drifting forward;
- forcing painful near-lockout angles.

### Workout-mode cues

**Elbows quiet • wrists neutral • curl into the strap**

## Rationale

The preacher curl appears in the historical seven-position sequence. Strap work expands the family without replacing the supported original.
