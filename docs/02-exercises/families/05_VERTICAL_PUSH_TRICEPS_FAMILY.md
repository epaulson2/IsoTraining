# Vertical Push / Triceps Family

## Status

**Validated family draft — modern extensions are explicitly labeled.**

## Family role

This family develops pressing strength and positional control with the shoulder in more elevated positions than the horizontal-push family. It also gives us a clean place for long-length triceps work using an adjustable non-elastic strap.

## Primary versions

### A. Dip Extreme Iso
- **Type:** Integrated
- **Provenance:** Schroeder-associated / historical derivative
- **Use:** Advanced whole-body pressing position

### B. Supported Dip Hold
- **Type:** Integrated regression
- **Provenance:** Schroeder-derived teaching regression
- **Use:** Learn Mode and shoulder-friendly depth acquisition

### C. Strap Overhead Triceps Iso
- **Type:** Targeted
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Long-length triceps loading with precise elbow angle and force intent

### D. Strap Overhead Press Iso
- **Type:** Targeted / integrated hybrid
- **Provenance:** Evidence-based extension
- **Use:** Shoulder-elevation pressing without requiring full bodyweight support

## Dip Extreme Iso

### Purpose

Train chest, triceps, anterior shoulder and scapular control in a demanding deep pressing position.

### Setup

- Use stable parallel bars or a secure dip station.
- Grip width should be fixed by the apparatus and comfortable for the shoulders.
- Lower only to a depth that preserves active shoulder control.
- Keep the rib cage and pelvis organized rather than aggressively arching to chase depth.
- Elbows track consistently rather than abruptly flaring.

### Active intent

This is not a passive bottom-of-dip hang.

1. Maintain active support through the hands and shoulder girdle.
2. Create downward/deeper intent only while the shoulders remain controlled.
3. Maintain triceps and chest tension rather than resting on anterior shoulder structures.
4. Keep the trunk organized and movement nearly invisible.

### Learn Mode

1. Learn top support with locked or nearly locked elbows.
2. Practice a shallow controlled descent.
3. Find the deepest pain-free position that still feels muscular.
4. Learn active shoulder support at that depth.
5. Add chest/triceps tension.
6. Add Extreme-Iso intent without dropping deeper passively.
7. Hold 6–10 seconds cleanly.
8. Press or step out under control.

### Common compensations

- anterior shoulder dumping;
- excessive depth chasing;
- shrugging;
- rib flare / lumbar extension;
- elbows changing path under fatigue.

### Workout-mode cues

**Active shoulders • ribs controlled • stay muscular at the bottom**

## Strap Overhead Triceps Iso

### Purpose

Target the long head of the triceps at a relatively long muscle length by combining shoulder flexion with elbow flexion.

### Setup

- Route the non-elastic strap so it resists elbow extension.
- Position upper arms overhead or near-overhead within comfortable shoulder range.
- Set elbow angle deep enough to lengthen the triceps without forcing the shoulder or neck.
- Keep wrists neutral and rib cage controlled.

### Active intent

Attempt to extend the elbows powerfully against the immovable strap while keeping the upper arms and trunk still.

### Learn Mode

1. Establish comfortable overhead arm position.
2. Set strap length and handle position.
3. Find a long but tolerable elbow-flexion angle.
4. Brace ribs and pelvis.
5. Build low-level elbow-extension force.
6. Increase force without letting elbows drift apart or forward.
7. Hold 6–10 seconds with stable geometry.
8. Reduce force gradually before exiting.

### Workout-mode cues

**Upper arms fixed • ribs down • extend hard into the strap**

## Evidence / rationale

The broader isometric literature supports manipulating muscle length, intensity and intent depending on the desired adaptation. Long-muscle-length isometrics are especially relevant to the targeted strap version, but this remains a modern extension rather than a Schroeder-original exercise.
